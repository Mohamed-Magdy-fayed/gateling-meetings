## Decision

**Not building AI summaries yet.** The feature is cheap to build and only
profitable on the cheapest pipeline. But its only purpose is getting people
to upgrade, and there are no outside free users to upgrade yet. The 1–2 weeks
go to getting outside users first.

## Problem Statement

The ask was "can we add AI summaries?" The honest trigger: a **reason for free
users to pay for Pro**. It's a business lever, not a fix for a pain users feel.

## Demand Evidence

- Real users today are the owner (client meetings, interviews) and the owner's
  wife (English classes, interviews).
- After a call, users "just leave." After interviews, the decision is made "on the
  spot." Nobody is scrambling for notes. **No demand evidence for summaries.**
- No outside user or customer has asked for them.

## Status Quo

No notes and no follow-up. The meeting is the whole point. Interview decisions
happen right away, before any summary would arrive.

## Cost vs revenue (the numbers that decided it)

Assumes ~50 EGP = $1. Re-check the rate and LiveKit egress pricing before
reviving this.

**Revenue per Pro seat:** 149 EGP sticker ([tiers.ts:57](../../src/features/billing/tiers.ts))
→ ~131 after 14% VAT → **~124 EGP net** after Paymob fees.

**Cost per summarized 45-minute meeting:**

| Pipeline | EGP / meeting | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed audio → Gemini Flash direct (no separate STT) | ~3–5 | Only viable option |
| Mixed audio → STT ($0.0025–0.008/min) → Gemini summary | ~8–20 | Loses money for a heavy Pro user |
| Live per-speaker STT (cost × participants) | ~50–150 | Never at EGP prices |

Plus LiveKit Cloud egress, roughly $0.02–0.15 per meeting (an estimate, not
checked against the invoice).

- A typical heavy user (~15 meetings/month) costs ~45–75 EGP on the direct
  pipeline, about 50% margin on a seat. On the STT pipeline: 120–300 EGP, a loss.
- A free taste of 3 summaries/month costs ~10–15 EGP per free org. At a 1-in-10
  upgrade rate, it pays back in about 2 months.
- **Revenue today: 0 EGP.** No outside free orgs exist to upgrade.

## Premises agreed in session (carry forward when revived)

1. It's a business lever, not a pain fix. Success = upgrades or signups.
2. Gate by count (2–3 free/month, ~20/month on Pro), not by plan alone, so free
   users see the value before they hit the wall.
3. The recap is emailed to **participants** (students, candidates, clients), not
   just the host. With few users, distribution is worth more than conversion.
4. Explicit per-meeting consent: the host turns it on and everyone sees an
   indicator. [privacy.ts:34](../../src/features/legal/content/privacy.ts)
   currently promises audio is never recorded or stored. That promise has to be
   rewritten, and the transcription/LLM provider added to the processor list.
5. Arabic and English must both work.

Premises were not formally confirmed. The owner asked for the cost/revenue
check instead and parked the feature after seeing it.

## Approaches considered

- **Cheap pipeline + caps:** recommended if building. Not chosen: no one to
  convert yet.
- **Test on ourselves first (host-only, flag-gated):** not chosen. The owner's
  household doesn't need summaries, so the test would only measure cost.
- **Raise Pro price with it:** not chosen. Raising a price with zero outside
  payers tests nothing.

## Revival trigger

Reopen this doc when **any** of these is true:

- There are ≥10 outside orgs on Free that have each held a meeting in the
  last 30 days (someone to convert).
- An outside user or prospect asks for AI notes, or names them as the reason
  they won't switch to Gateling.
- A teacher or business pilot would pay for a participant recap (e.g.
  post-class vocabulary and homework sent to students).

When revived, start from the direct-to-Gemini pipeline. It reuses the existing
`@tanstack/ai-gemini` companion setup, the companion's monthly-cap pattern in
`companionConfig`, and the Inngest LiveKit webhook as the "meeting ended" hook.

## The Assignment

Before writing any more features, get **5 outside people who aren't family**
to run a real meeting on Gateling this month. Start with English teachers your
wife knows. For each one, write down what they did after the call ended. That
list, not a competitor checklist, decides whether summaries come back.
